SPECIFICATIONS & QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Standard mall t-shirts typically weigh 130–160 GSM, resulting in clinging, sheer transparency, and bacon collars after a few washes. Our 240 GSM and 280 GSM heavyweight cotton provides structural drape, stays opaque, keeps its clean boxy line, and uses heavy double-stitched collar ribbing that will never stretch or curl.

Because our fabric is pre-washed and sanforized against shrinkage, maintenance is simple: Machine wash cold (30°C / 85°F) inside-out with like colors. Hang dry or tumble dry low. Do not iron directly over silicone or screen-printed graphics.

Our tees are intentionally designed with an oversized, drop-shoulder silhouette. We recommend ordering your true standard size for that relaxed streetwear look. If you desire a traditional fitted silhouette, size down one size.

Domestic express shipping takes 2-3 business days. International express takes 4-7 business days with end-to-end tracking. All orders over $100 receive automatic Free Worldwide Express Shipping.
